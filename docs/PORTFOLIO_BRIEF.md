# Portfolio brief

## Owner

Artkin Carreon

## Positioning

AI Automation Specialist who designs practical workflow automation,
integrations, and AI-assisted systems that reduce repetitive work for small
businesses.

## Primary audience

Small-business owners and decision-makers evaluating Artkin's ability to map,
build, and verify reliable automation.

## Visitor journey

1. Understand Artkin's specialization.
2. Review verified projects and applied evidence.
3. Understand the working method and constraints.
4. Start an automation inquiry.

## Current public scope

- Home with Projects, Systems, AI Signal, Credentials, and Contact sections.
- Work index and individual project routes.
- Credentials, About, Contact, and Privacy pages.
- Responsive desktop sidebar and mobile navigation.

## Current private scope

Admin interface mockups exist for projects, credentials, inquiries, and
settings. They are frontend-only and are not authenticated or connected to a
database.

## Evidence rules

- Publish only verified projects and credentials.
- Never invent clients, technologies, metrics, outcomes, testimonials, or work
  history.
- Keep missing records empty rather than filling them with sample content.
- Do not claim a contact submission was delivered unless the service was tested.

## Out of scope until explicitly requested

- Database, authentication, storage, API, email delivery, AI processing, or n8n.
- Articles, public accounts, multi-editor administration, and automatic replies.
